## 🚀 What is PulseLoop?

**PulseLoop** is a memory-driven customer feedback intelligence platform that transforms customer feedback from isolated records into meaningful product intelligence.

Traditional feedback systems often store customer comments without understanding their historical context.

PulseLoop takes a different approach.

It uses **Hindsight Memory as its core memory layer** to remember historical customer feedback, retrieve relevant past experiences, connect them with current questions, identify recurring problems, understand how issues change over time, and recommend what product teams should do next.

> **PulseLoop doesn't just remember feedback — it uses memory to understand what the feedback means over time.**

---

# 🧠 Hindsight Memory — The Hero of PulseLoop

Hindsight Memory is the central intelligence layer of PulseLoop.

Instead of treating every feedback message as a completely new piece of information, PulseLoop can connect today's question with relevant historical customer experiences.
